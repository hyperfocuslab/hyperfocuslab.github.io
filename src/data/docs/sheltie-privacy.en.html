Effective date: November 23, 2026

Sheltie is an iPhone and Android app by Hyperfocus Lab. It connects to a server you install on your own computer (a Mac or a Windows PC) and shows you the coding agents running there. This policy says what data the app handles and where it goes.

What stays on your phone

  • The address of your computer’s server and its access token. The token is kept in the iOS Keychain, or on Android encrypted with a key in the Android Keystore.
  • App settings.

Conversations, prompts and terminal screens are loaded from your computer only while you look at them and are not saved by the app.

What goes to your computer

Everything you do in the app (answers, messages, new tasks) goes to the server on your own computer over your Tailscale network. That server is software you run; we have no access to it or to anything on your computer.

What goes through our push relay

To send notifications, Apple and Google require a key that only we hold, so notifications pass through a small relay we run on Cloudflare Workers:

  • Your device’s push token, which Apple (iPhone) or Firebase Cloud Messaging (Android) issues so a notification can reach your phone.
  • The notification itself: the terminal’s name and a short status line such as “approve Write: index.html” or “done”.

The relay takes the device push token and the notification your computer’s server sends and passes them straight on to Apple Push Notification service or Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging. It does not store the token or the notification text, and does not write either to logs.

Cloudflare (hosting the relay), Apple and Google (delivering the notification) process this data only to deliver notifications.

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone.

This website

We use Google Analytics for visit statistics on this site (hyperfocuslab.app). It is separate from the app.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code your computer shows. Images are not saved or sent.

Microphone

The microphone is used only when you dictate a reply. Your phone’s speech recognition service (Apple or Google) turns it into text; the app does not save or send the audio itself.

Children

Sheltie is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Clear the server address and token in the app’s settings, or delete the app, to erase what it stores on your phone. On a Mac, install.sh --uninstall stops the server and deleting ~/.config/herdr-remote removes its settings and the list of registered devices. On Windows, uninstall with the install script (see the support page), then delete %USERPROFILE%\.config\herdr-remote.

Inquiries sent from the app

When you send an inquiry from inside the app, the following is delivered to Hyperfocus Lab.

  • What is sent: the text of your inquiry, a reply email address if you enter one, the app version, OS version, device model, language, and an identifier the app creates for the device (identifierForVendor on iOS).
  • Where it goes: it passes through a Google Apps Script server, is stored in a Google spreadsheet managed by Hyperfocus Lab, and is forwarded by email to support@hyperfocuslab.app.
  • What it is used for: only to answer your inquiry.
  • Deleting it: email support@hyperfocuslab.app and we will delete your inquiry.
