An iPhone app that records all day and does transcription, speaker labels and summaries on your iPhone.

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 26 or later
  • Summaries and next steps work on devices with Apple Intelligence turned on
  • Apple Watch recording needs watchOS 26 or later
  • Version 1.0 is free, with no in-app purchases

FAQ

Where do my recordings go?

Transcription, speaker labels and summaries all run on your iPhone, and recordings and text are not sent to the developer's servers. Data leaves the device only when you turn it on or choose it (iCloud sync, sharing and export, iCloud links). Details are in the Privacy Policy.

When are recordings deleted?

After 30 days by default they move to Recently Deleted, and 7 days later they are erased. You can change the period in Settings, and starred recordings are not moved. Deleting the app also deletes the records stored on the device.

Are to-dos added to Reminders automatically?

No. You confirm each to-do from a summary before it goes to Reminders, and each event before it goes to Calendar.

I do not see a summary.

Summaries, topics and next steps work on devices with Apple Intelligence turned on.
