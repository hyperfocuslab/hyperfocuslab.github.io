App Store subscriptions and restoring purchases

If Pro isn’t active after you bought it, sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase, then tap “Restore Purchases” on Peakly’s subscription screen.

You can change or cancel your subscription in Settings on your iPhone → Apple Account → Subscriptions.
