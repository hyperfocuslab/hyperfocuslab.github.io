Information we collect

Cockatoo does not collect personal information. There is no account, no developer server, and no analytics tool or advertising SDK. The developer cannot see your recordings or conversations.

What is processed only on your device

  • Your voice (recordings) is never sent to the developer or any outside server.
  • Transcription uses Apple's on-device speech recognition.
  • Pronunciation scoring runs on your iPhone with a pronunciation model bundled in the app.
  • The voice that reads AI replies aloud, and translations into your device language, are also made on the device. If the translation language pack for that language is not installed, the on-device model of Apple Intelligence is used.

Text sent to Apple Private Cloud Compute

For the AI to reply in English and correct your sentences, the transcribed text of what you said and the earlier part of that conversation are sent to Apple Intelligence. Requests are processed by the on-device model or by Apple's Private Cloud Compute.

  • Only text is sent. Audio files are never sent.
  • Notes you write about your day to talk about are sent the same way, as text only. Leave out anything you do not want to send, such as other people's names or details about your work.
  • Apple states that data sent to Private Cloud Compute is used only to fulfil the request and is not stored. The developer does not receive and cannot see this text.

What is stored and when it is deleted

  • Conversations, transcribed text, corrected sentences, pronunciation results, your recordings and your English level setting are kept in the app's storage on your device. If device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) is on, this data is included in your backup like other app data, so your recordings move with you to a new device.
  • Recordings are kept with no time limit so you can compare them with earlier ones. The app only cleans up recording files that are not linked to any record.
  • Swiping to delete an item in Review or Pronunciation habits deletes that record and its recording from the device.
  • Deleting the app also deletes everything it stored on the device.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record what you say in English, transcribe it and check your pronunciation. Recording happens only while you hold the button.
  • Speech recognition: to turn what you said into text on the device.
