An iPhone app where you talk with an AI in English, get your sentences corrected, and get your pronunciation checked word by word.

What you need

  • An iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence
  • iOS 27 or later

FAQ

Where do my recordings go?

Recordings are never sent to the developer or any outside server. Transcription and pronunciation scoring also run on the iPhone. The only thing the app sends out is text sent to Apple Intelligence to write AI replies. If device backup is on, recordings are included in that backup. Details are in the privacy policy.

What language are explanations and translations in?

They follow your iPhone language. To use the app in a different language, go to Settings > Apps > Cockatoo > Language. Supported: 한국어, English, 日本語, 繁體中文, Español, Português (Brasil), Deutsch and Français; any other language shows English. When the app language is English, there is no translate button.

How do I delete my records?

Swipe to delete an item in Review or Pronunciation habits, and that record and its recording are deleted from the device. Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on the device.
