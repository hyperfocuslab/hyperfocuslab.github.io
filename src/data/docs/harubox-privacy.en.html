Effective date: November 23, 2026 · Developer: Hyperfocus Lab

Information we collect

Harubox does not collect personal information. There is no account, no developer server, and no analytics tool or ad SDK. The developer cannot see your recordings or transcripts.

What is processed only on your device

  • Recording, transcription, speaker separation, summaries and to-do extraction all happen on your iPhone. No audio or text leaves the device for processing.
  • Transcription uses Apple’s on-device speech recognition, summaries use Apple Intelligence’s on-device model, and speaker separation uses a model bundled in the app.

What is stored and when it is deleted

  • Recording files, transcripts, speaker names, summaries, notes and bookmarks are kept in the app’s storage on your device.
  • Recording files are large, so they are excluded from device backups (iCloud Backup and computer backups).
  • By default, recordings move to “Recently Deleted” after 30 days, and 7 days later the audio, transcript and summary are erased together. You can change the retention period in Settings, and recordings you starred are not moved. Recordings you delete yourself go through the same 7 days.
  • Deleting the app also deletes the records stored on the device.

When data leaves your device

The following happens only when you choose or turn it on.

  • iCloud sync: recordings and records are stored in the iCloud of your own Apple account and kept in step with your other devices. Apple stores them, and the developer cannot see them. Data left in iCloud can be deleted in iPhone Settings > your name > iCloud.
  • Sharing and export: text, PDF or audio files are sent to the app or person you pick.
  • iCloud link: if you choose “iCloud link” when sharing, a copy of that recording or transcript is placed in the app folder of your iCloud Drive, and anyone with the link can download it. You can view and delete the copy in the Files app > iCloud Drive.
  • Reminders and Calendar: only the to-dos and events you confirmed from a summary are added. After that, they follow the settings of the Reminders and Calendar apps.

Apple Watch

Files recorded on the watch are moved only to your iPhone, and transcription and summaries happen on the iPhone. A piece is deleted from the watch only after the iPhone confirms it received it.

Permissions

  • Microphone: used to record. While recording, iOS shows an orange dot on the screen.
  • Speech recognition: used to turn recorded speech into text on the device.
  • Reminders and Calendar: requested only when you review a to-do or event and tap “Add.” For Calendar, the app asks for write-only access.
  • Notifications: used for a to-do you dictate during recording by starting with “assistant”, and to tell you when a watch recording was interrupted. These are notifications shown on the device; the developer sends none.

Payment

Harubox is free and has no in-app purchases, so it does not handle payment details such as card information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
