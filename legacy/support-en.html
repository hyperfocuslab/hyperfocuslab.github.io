App Store subscriptions and restoring purchases

If Pro is missing after a purchase, sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase, then tap “Restore Purchases” on Peakly’s subscription screen.

To change or cancel a subscription, open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your Apple Account → Subscriptions, then select Peakly.
